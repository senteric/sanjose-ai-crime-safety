# SJSU SafetyMap: AI for campus safety incident visibility gaps

Client: City of San José, Increasing Community Safety  
Team: James Doan, Rithvik Saladi, Taran Sahota, Eric Liao Aug–Oct 2026  
My part: I designed the AI-assisted reporting workflow and built the incident-reporting and campus safety heat-map prototype.

## The problem

SJSU students who live near or travel through campus and Downtown San José often lack timely, easy-to-understand information about where safety incidents are being reported. Existing information can arrive after an incident has already happened, while reporting itself can be unclear or time-consuming.

## What we built

- Built an AI-assisted incident reporting flow where users can upload a photo and short description, then review AI-generated details such as incident type and location before submitting.
- Created an interactive, color-coded heat map that combines approved incident reports to show areas with higher concentrations of reported safety activity.
- Added a human review process where an analyst can approve, deny, or remove reports before they affect the public heat map, rather than allowing AI to make final safety decisions automatically.

## What I would do next

The biggest remaining gap is validating whether students would submit enough accurate and timely reports for the heat map to represent safety patterns reliably. Next, I would test the prototype with SJSU students and improve the system's location detection and safer-route recommendations based on their feedback.
